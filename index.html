📜 Culture History

🌿 Santali Culture 🌿 Bhumij Culture

Santali culture is one of India's oldest tribal traditions, rich in music, dance, language, festivals and community values.

Santali culture is mainly found in Jharkhand, Odisha, West Bengal and Bihar. Major festivals include Karam, Sohrai and Baha. Traditional instruments like Madal, Tumdak and Flute are widely used in dance and music. The Santali language is written in the Ol Chiki script and reflects a deep connection with nature, unity and cultural heritage.

Bhumij culture represents simplicity, unity and a strong relationship with nature through traditional festivals, music and dance.

The Bhumij community mainly lives in Odisha, Jharkhand, West Bengal and Bihar. Festivals like Karam and Tusu play an important role in their cultural life. Traditional dance, Nagara, Dhol and local folk music reflect their rich heritage and continue to preserve their identity for future generations.
