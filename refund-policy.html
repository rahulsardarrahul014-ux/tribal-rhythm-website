Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

Thank you for choosing TribalRhythm. This Refund & Cancellation Policy explains the conditions under which refunds or cancellations may be requested for registrations, competitions, events and ticket bookings.

1. Registration Fees

Registration fees paid for competitions, dance classes or events are generally non-refundable once payment has been successfully completed.

2. Ticket Cancellation

  • Tickets cannot be cancelled after successful payment.
  • Each Ticket ID is unique and cannot be transferred to another person.
  • Lost or deleted tickets can be re-issued after identity verification.

3. Duplicate Payment

If your account is charged more than once for the same booking due to a technical issue, please contact us with your payment details. After verification, the duplicate payment will be refunded to the original payment method.

4. Event Cancellation by TribalRhythm

If TribalRhythm cancels an event or competition due to unforeseen circumstances, participants may receive:

  • A full refund, or
  • The option to transfer the registration to another event.

5. Failed Transactions

If payment fails but money is deducted from your account, the amount is usually automatically reversed by your bank or payment provider. If the amount is not reversed within the expected time, please contact your bank first and then contact TribalRhythm with your transaction details.

6. Refund Processing Time

Approved refunds are generally processed within 5–10 business days. The exact credit time depends on your bank, UPI provider or card issuer.

7. Non-Refundable Situations

  • User entered incorrect information.
  • User missed the event.
  • User voluntarily cancelled participation.
  • Violation of competition rules.
  • Account suspension due to fraudulent activity.

8. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund (where applicable), please contact us with:

  • Full Name
  • Registered Email
  • Mobile Number
  • Ticket ID
  • Payment ID
  • Reason for refund request
