Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last Updated: August 2026
Thank you for choosing TribalRhythm. This Refund & Cancellation Policy explains the conditions applicable to payments made for registrations, competitions, dance classes, events and ticket bookings. TribalRhythm is powered by Zentro Nex.

1 Registration Fees

Registration fees paid for competitions, dance classes, workshops or events are generally non-refundable once the payment has been successfully completed.

2 Ticket Cancellation

  • Tickets cannot normally be cancelled after successful payment.
  • Each Ticket ID is unique and may be linked to the registered participant.
  • Tickets should not be transferred or resold without written authorization.
  • Lost or deleted tickets may be re-issued after identity verification.

3 Duplicate Payment

If your bank account, card or UPI account is charged more than once for the same booking due to a technical issue, please contact TribalRhythm with the relevant transaction details. After verification, the duplicate payment may be refunded to the original payment method.

4 Event Cancellation by TribalRhythm

If TribalRhythm cancels an event, competition, workshop or program due to unforeseen circumstances, operational reasons or other circumstances beyond reasonable control, TribalRhythm may provide one of the following options, subject to the applicable event terms:

  • Full or partial refund, where applicable.
  • Transfer of registration to a rescheduled event.
  • Credit or alternative participation option, where applicable.

5 Failed Transactions

If a payment fails but money is deducted from your bank account, the amount may automatically be reversed by your bank, UPI provider or payment gateway. If the amount is not reversed within the applicable banking/payment-provider timeline, please contact your bank or payment provider first and then contact TribalRhythm with transaction details.

6 Refund Processing Time

Approved refunds are generally initiated within 5–10 business days after verification and approval. The actual time required for the amount to appear in your account may vary depending on your bank, UPI provider, card issuer or payment gateway.

7 Non-Refundable Situations

  • Incorrect information entered by the user.
  • User does not attend the event.
  • User voluntarily withdraws from participation.
  • Violation of competition or event rules.
  • Fraudulent, suspicious or unauthorized activity associated with the account.
  • Services or participation already provided to the user.

8 Refund Eligibility

Refund requests are reviewed individually. Submission of a refund request does not automatically guarantee approval. TribalRhythm may request additional information or documents to verify the transaction and eligibility.

9 How to Request a Refund

Where a refund is applicable, please contact us with the following information:

  • Full Name
  • Registered Email Address
  • Registered Mobile Number
  • Ticket ID, if applicable
  • Payment ID / Transaction ID
  • Date of Payment
  • Reason for Refund Request

10 Payment Gateway

Payments may be processed through third-party payment gateways or financial service providers. TribalRhythm does not control the processing timelines of banks, UPI providers, card issuers or payment gateways.

Important: Please keep your payment receipt, Payment ID, Ticket ID and transaction details until your registration or booking has been successfully completed.

  • पुनर्निर्धारित कार्यक्रम में रजिस्ट्रेशन ट्रांसफर।

  • धोखाधड़ी या संदिग्ध गतिविधि।
  • सेवा या भागीदारी पहले ही प्रदान की जा चुकी हो।

ପ୍ରତ୍ୟେକ ରିଫଣ୍ଡ ଅନୁରୋଧକୁ ଅଲଗା ଭାବରେ ଯାଞ୍ଚ କରାଯିବ। କେବଳ ରିଫଣ୍ଡ ଅନୁରୋଧ କରିବା ଦ୍ୱାରା ରିଫଣ୍ଡ ନିଶ୍ଚିତ ହୁଏ ନାହିଁ। ଯାଞ୍ଚ ପାଇଁ ଅତିରିକ୍ତ ସୂଚନା କିମ୍ବା ଡକ୍ୟୁମେଣ୍ଟ ମଗାଯାଇପାରେ।

9 ରିଫଣ୍ଡ କିପରି ଅନୁରୋଧ କରିବେ

ଯେଉଁଠାରେ ରିଫଣ୍ଡ ପ୍ରଯୁଜ୍ୟ, ନିମ୍ନଲିଖିତ ସୂଚନା ସହିତ ଆମ ସହିତ ଯୋଗାଯୋଗ କରନ୍ତୁ:

  • ପୂର୍ଣ୍ଣ ନାମ
  • ରେଜିଷ୍ଟର୍ଡ ଇମେଲ୍
  • ରେଜିଷ୍ଟର୍ଡ ମୋବାଇଲ୍ ନମ୍ବର
  • Ticket ID
  • Payment ID / Transaction ID
  • ପେମେଣ୍ଟ ତାରିଖ
  • ରିଫଣ୍ଡର କାରଣ

10 ପେମେଣ୍ଟ ଗେଟୱେ

ପେମେଣ୍ଟ ପାଇଁ ଥାର୍ଡ-ପାର୍ଟି ପେମେଣ୍ଟ ଗେଟୱେ କିମ୍ବା ଆର୍ଥିକ ସେବା ପ୍ରଦାନକାରୀ ବ୍ୟବହାର କରାଯାଇପାରେ। ବ୍ୟାଙ୍କ, UPI ପ୍ରଦାନକାରୀ, କାର୍ଡ ଜାରିକର୍ତ୍ତା କିମ୍ବା ପେମେଣ୍ଟ ଗେଟୱେର ପ୍ରୋସେସିଂ ସମୟ TribalRhythm ର ନିୟନ୍ତ୍ରଣରେ ନଥାଏ।
