Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

Thank you for choosing TribalRhythm. TribalRhythm is an online platform for dance competitions, cultural events, registrations and digital ticket booking. TribalRhythm does not sell or deliver physical products. All applicable services are provided digitally.

1. Nature of Delivery

TribalRhythm provides digital services including:

  • Competition Registration
  • Event Ticket Booking
  • Digital Ticket ID
  • Email Confirmation
  • SMS Confirmation
  • Online Participation Details
  • Digital Certificates, where applicable

2. Delivery Time

After successful payment confirmation, your digital ticket, registration confirmation or booking details are generally delivered instantly or within a few minutes.

In rare situations caused by internet issues, payment gateway delays, technical problems or scheduled maintenance, delivery may take up to 24 hours.

3. Delivery Method

Your booking or registration confirmation may be delivered through:

  • Email Confirmation
  • SMS Notification
  • Website Dashboard
  • Ticket ID displayed after successful payment
  • Other digital communication methods provided by TribalRhythm

4. No Physical Shipping

TribalRhythm does not ship any physical goods, products or printed tickets. No courier, postal or logistics service is involved in the delivery of our digital services.

5. Incorrect Information

Users are responsible for providing accurate and complete information, including their email address and mobile number, during registration or booking. TribalRhythm is not responsible for delivery failures caused by incorrect, incomplete or outdated information submitted by the user.

6. Failed or Delayed Delivery

If you do not receive your confirmation within the expected time, please:

  • Check your Spam or Junk email folder.
  • Verify your registered email address.
  • Verify your registered mobile number.
  • Check your TribalRhythm account/dashboard.
  • Contact TribalRhythm support.

7. Service Availability

Delivery times may be affected by scheduled maintenance, internet outages, payment gateway processing delays, server issues, third-party service interruptions or other force majeure events beyond our reasonable control.

8. Payment Confirmation

Digital delivery is initiated only after successful payment confirmation where payment is required. If a payment is pending, failed, reversed or not confirmed by the payment gateway, the associated digital service may not be delivered until the payment status is successfully verified.

TribalRhythm निम्नलिखित डिजिटल सेवाएं प्रदान करता है:

  • प्रतियोगिता रजिस्ट्रेशन

आपकी बुकिंग या रजिस्ट्रेशन पुष्टि निम्न माध्यमों से दी जा सकती है:

  • ईमेल कन्फर्मेशन
  • SMS सूचना
  • वेबसाइट डैशबोर्ड

5. गलत जानकारी

रजिस्ट्रेशन या बुकिंग के समय सही और पूर्ण जानकारी देना उपयोगकर्ता की जिम्मेदारी है, जिसमें ईमेल पता और मोबाइल नंबर भी शामिल हैं। उपयोगकर्ता द्वारा गलत, अधूरी या पुरानी जानकारी दिए जाने के कारण होने वाली डिलीवरी समस्या के लिए TribalRhythm जिम्मेदार नहीं होगा।

  • Spam/Junk ईमेल फोल्डर जांचें।
  • अपना रजिस्टर्ड ईमेल पता जांचें।

  • TribalRhythm सपोर्ट से संपर्क करें।
