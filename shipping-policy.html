Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

Thank you for choosing TribalRhythm. TribalRhythm is an online platform for dance competitions, cultural events, registrations and digital ticket booking. We do not sell or deliver physical products. All services are provided digitally.

1. Nature of Delivery

TribalRhythm provides only digital services including:

  • Competition Registration
  • Event Ticket Booking
  • Digital Ticket ID
  • Email Confirmation
  • SMS Confirmation
  • Online Participation Details

2. Delivery Time

After successful payment confirmation, your digital ticket, registration confirmation or booking details are generally delivered instantly or within a few minutes.

In rare situations due to internet issues, payment gateway delays or server maintenance, delivery may take up to 24 hours.

3. Delivery Method

Your booking confirmation may be delivered through:

  • Email Confirmation
  • SMS Notification
  • Website Dashboard
  • Ticket ID displayed after successful payment

4. No Physical Shipping

TribalRhythm does not ship any physical goods, products or printed tickets. No courier, postal or logistics service is involved.

5. Incorrect Information

Users are responsible for providing the correct email address and mobile number during registration. TribalRhythm is not responsible for delivery failures caused by incorrect information submitted by the user.

6. Failed Delivery

If you do not receive your confirmation within the expected time, please:

  • Check your Spam/Junk email folder.
  • Verify your registered email address.
  • Verify your registered mobile number.
  • Contact TribalRhythm support.

7. Service Availability

Delivery times may be affected by scheduled maintenance, internet outages, payment gateway processing delays, or force majeure events beyond our control.
